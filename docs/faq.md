# FAQ

**Is this free?**
Yes. It's MIT-licensed and needs no paid data. You need a Claude plan that includes Claude Code.

**Will it tell me which stock to buy?**
No. It explains the market, a company, risks and the math so *you* can decide. For personal advice, consult a
SEBI-registered investment adviser. You can verify registration on [sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in/) under recognised intermediaries.

**Does it connect to Groww, Zerodha or any broker?**
No. It never asks for logins and never places orders. You trade in your own broker app.

**How accurate is the data?**
Prices come from Yahoo Finance and can be ~15 minutes delayed. Fundamentals and news come from web search with
sources, which you should check. Charges in the position sizer are estimates; your contract note is the final word.

**Does it work for US stocks?**
These skills focus on India. The navigator routes US questions to
[tradermonty/claude-trading-skills](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills) if you've installed them.

**Can I use it in Hindi?**
Yes. Write in Hindi or Hinglish and Claude replies the same way.

**Is my data safe?**
The scripts only download public prices. Nothing about you is sent anywhere by this project. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

**Is there a paid version, Telegram group or tip service?**
**No.** Anyone claiming so isn't connected to this project.

**Can I contribute?**
Yes, please. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
