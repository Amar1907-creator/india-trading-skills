# Reading the Market Pulse: a plain-English guide

| Term | What it means | Why it matters |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| Nifty 50 | The 50 biggest companies on NSE | The main "temperature" of the Indian market |
| Bank Nifty | The biggest, most traded bank stocks | Banks are the largest sector, so Nifty rarely rises without them |
| Sensex | 30 big companies on BSE | Moves very similarly to Nifty |
| Nifty Next 50 | Companies ranked 51-100 | Tomorrow's large-caps |
| Midcap 150 / Smallcap 250 | Medium and small companies | Rise faster in good times and fall harder in bad times |
| 50-day / 200-day average | Average closing price of the last 50 / 200 trading days | Price above both usually means a healthy trend |
| RSI (14) | A 0-100 speed meter of recent moves | Above 70 means it ran up fast, below 30 means it fell fast. Neither is a signal by itself |
| India VIX | Expected volatility over the next 30 days | Higher means bigger swings and more fear |
| GIFT Nifty | Nifty futures traded in GIFT City from early morning | Hints at how Nifty may open |
| FII / DII | Foreign / Domestic institutional investors (big funds) | Heavy FII selling often weighs on large-caps |

## India VIX zones used

- Below 13: calm (low fear; sometimes too relaxed)
- 13-18: normal
- 18-25: nervous
- 25 and above: high fear

## Global cues and their typical effect on India

- **US markets up strongly overnight** → often a positive start for India (not always).
- **Rupee weakens (USD/INR rises)** → good for IT and pharma exporters, bad for importers and oil companies.
- **Crude oil rises** → hurts oil marketing companies (BPCL, HPCL, IOC), paints, tyres and airlines, and raises inflation worries.

These are tendencies, not rules.
