# Indian stock market glossary (simple words)

## Basics
- **Share / stock**: a small piece of ownership in a company. Owning 1 share of Reliance makes you a tiny owner of Reliance.
- **NSE / BSE**: the two main stock exchanges in India, the "markets" where shares are bought and sold.
- **Nifty 50**: an index of 50 big companies on NSE. It's like the class average marks: it tells you how the big companies did overall.
- **Sensex**: an index of 30 big companies on BSE.
- **Demat account**: an electronic locker that holds your shares. Groww and Zerodha open one for you.
- **Broker**: the company that places your orders on the exchange (Groww, Zerodha, Upstox, Angel One).
- **SEBI**: the government regulator that makes the rules and protects investors.
- **Market hours**: pre-open 9:00-9:15 AM, normal trading 9:15 AM-3:30 PM IST, Monday-Friday, except exchange holidays.
- **T+1 settlement**: shares bought today reach your demat account the next working day.

## Orders
- **Delivery (CNC)**: buy and keep the shares as long as you like.
- **Intraday (MIS)**: buy and sell on the same day. The broker auto-closes the position before market close.
- **Market order**: buy or sell immediately at the current price.
- **Limit order**: buy or sell only at your chosen price or better.
- **Stop-loss (SL)**: an order that sells automatically if the price falls to your chosen level, to limit the loss.
- **GTT (Good Till Triggered)**: an order that waits (up to about a year) until the price reaches your level.

## Reading a stock
- **Market cap**: share price × number of shares, the company's total value. Large-cap, mid-cap and small-cap are size groups (set by SEBI rankings).
- **P/E ratio**: price ÷ earnings per share, i.e. how many years of current profit you're paying for. Compare with similar companies, not across sectors.
- **EPS**: profit per share.
- **ROE**: profit ÷ shareholders' money, i.e. how well the company uses owners' money. Above 15% is generally considered good.
- **Debt-to-equity**: loans ÷ owners' money. Lower is generally safer (except for banks).
- **Dividend**: a part of profit paid to shareholders.
- **Promoter**: the founder or owner group. **Pledge** means they borrowed against their shares, which is risky if it's high.
- **52-week high/low**: the highest and lowest price in the last year.

## Charts and indicators
- **Candlestick**: one bar showing the open, high, low and close for a day. Green means it closed higher, red means it closed lower.
- **Support / resistance**: price levels where buying or selling has often appeared before.
- **Moving average (50-day, 200-day)**: the average price over recent days. Price above the 200-day average suggests a long-term uptrend.
- **RSI**: a 0-100 speed meter. Above 70 means it went up fast (overbought), below 30 means it fell fast (oversold). Neither is a signal by itself.
- **Volume**: how many shares changed hands. A big move on big volume is more meaningful.
- **ATR**: how much a stock typically moves in a day. It helps set a sensible stop-loss.

## Market mood
- **Bull / bear market**: rising / falling for a long time.
- **India VIX**: the fear meter. Higher means bigger expected swings.
- **FII / DII**: foreign and domestic institutional investors (mutual funds, insurance companies). Their buying and selling moves big stocks.
- **Upper / lower circuit**: the maximum the price can move in a day for some stocks. Trading may freeze there.
- **ASM / GSM**: NSE/BSE surveillance lists for risky or unusual stocks. Be extra careful with them.

## Derivatives (advanced; not for beginners)
- **F&O**: Futures and Options, contracts based on a stock or index price.
- **Lot size**: the fixed minimum quantity for one F&O contract. NSE revises it from time to time.
- **Expiry**: the date a contract ends.
- **Call / Put option**: the right to buy (call) or sell (put) at a set price. Buyers can lose the whole premium.
- **Margin / leverage**: trading a big position with a smaller deposit. Profits and losses are both magnified.
- **Option chain**: a table of all option prices and open interest for one stock or index.
- **Open interest (OI)**: the number of open contracts.
- **PCR (put-call ratio)**: puts OI ÷ calls OI, a sentiment gauge.

## Investing
- **SIP**: investing a fixed amount every month, in a mutual fund or stock.
- **Index fund / ETF**: a fund that simply copies an index like Nifty 50. It has low cost and is diversified.
- **IPO**: a company selling shares to the public for the first time.
- **STCG / LTCG**: short-term / long-term capital gains tax. Rates change in Budgets, so check current rates.
