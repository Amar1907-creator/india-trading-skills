# 4-week beginner learning path

Each lesson takes about 15 minutes. Tell the coach "start lesson 1".

## Week 1: How the market works
1. What a share is, NSE/BSE, Nifty and Sensex, market hours
2. Demat accounts, brokers, SEBI, and how money and shares move (T+1)
3. Delivery vs intraday, market vs limit orders. Practise in the app *without* placing an order
4. Reading a stock page in Groww: price, market cap, P/E, 52-week range

## Week 2: Is this a good company?
5. Revenue and profit growth: where to find them (Screener.in, annual report)
6. ROE, debt and promoter holding. Try **india-stock-explainer** on a company you know
7. Valuation: what P/E means, and why to compare with peers
8. Red flags: pledges, auditor exits, surveillance lists, tips from strangers

## Week 3: Price and timing basics
9. Candlesticks, trends, moving averages
10. RSI, volume and ATR, using the snapshot from india-stock-explainer
11. Market mood: run **india-market-pulse** and read every line
12. Sectors: run **india-sector-rotation** and understand the four labels

## Week 4: Protecting your money
13. Stop-loss and the 1% rule. Practise with **india-position-sizer**
14. Charges and taxes: why small trades are costly
15. Writing a trade plan before buying: entry, stop, target, reason
16. Why F&O and intraday are risky for beginners, and what SEBI found

After week 4: paper-trade (write trades down without real money) for at least a month before using real money,
and start small.
